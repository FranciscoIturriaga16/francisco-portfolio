HAVEN

One platform where communities run themselves — and members manage every community they belong to from a single account.

Role
Product design & full-stack development
Stack
React · TypeScript · Supabase
Timeline
[Start date] – Present
Platform
Mobile-first web app
01

The problem

Communities run on a patchwork of tools. Members struggle with scattered communication, outdated calendars, and a separate login for every community.

The result is confusion, wasted time, and missed opportunities for connection.

From a user interview

“We juggle spreadsheets, calls, and separate apps. It works, but it is far from simple.”

Community administrator, on managing reservations and updates
02

The goal

Give communities independence and simplicity: create and run a community in minutes, without a developer.

For administrators

Facilities, events, pricing, members, guests, dining, announcements and insights — from one dashboard.

For members

One account for every community — switch between them to book courts, join events, invite guests and stay informed.

03

The audience

Two groups that depend on each other.

Primary
Administrators

Property managers, club administrators and HOA boards who need a professional way to run their community without outside technical help.

Secondary
Members

People who take part in daily community life and want one easy, unified way to interact with every community they belong to.

04

Discovery

Early research and interviews, a competitive review, and low-fidelity prototypes tested with both user groups surfaced three insights.

  1. A
    Admins are frustrated by fragmentation

    Many rely on disconnected platforms or expensive custom-built websites.

  2. B
    Members want everything in one place

    Bookings, events and announcements — convenient and accessible.

  3. C
    Flexibility and control are key

    Admins want to manage everything themselves; members want a modern experience that feels effortless.

05

The solution

Everything a community needs, in one cohesive system.

Administrators
  • Create and customize a community without a developer
  • Manage facilities, events, pricing and memberships
  • Track bookings, payments and activity in real time
  • Publish announcements, dining updates and guest policies
Members
  • Access every community with one HAVEN account
  • Switch communities to book, register or invite guests
  • Manage reservations and payments in one place
  • Optionally create a community of their own
06

Where it stands

HAVEN is in active development, built to scale from a small tennis club to a large residential community.

Administrators get full control through a dashboard that updates in real time; members get an intuitive, mobile-friendly interface.
